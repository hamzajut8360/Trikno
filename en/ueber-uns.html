About Us: TRIKNO (formerly Knobel)

TRIKNO AG, based in Felben near Frauenfeld, Switzerland – Swiss engineering precision and flexible solutions for chocolate and confectionery artisans and industrial leaders worldwide.

Heritage & Excellence

We are the Swiss experts in chocolate machinery

We are the Swiss experts in the highest-quality chocolate and confectionery machinery on the market: from small depositing machines designed to support artisanal production right through to complete production lines for the world’s largest chocolate manufacturers.

Our extensive product portfolio includes innovative machines for decorating, nuts & fruit, one shot, free-pressing and sandwich applications, as well as our unique ColdPress process. We also operate a unique Research, Development & Test laboratory and offer comprehensive retrofit solutions.
