Retrofit & Modernization

Upgrade, expand, and modernize third-party lines and TRIKNO systems with state-of-the-art technology.

Modernize Production Lines & Extend Machine Life

Realise new products. Optimise existing product processes. Increase quality. You can upgrade, expand, and modernize your existing third-party installations with high-precision TRIKNO depositing systems.

Seize the opportunity to elevate your production lines to cutting-edge standards without the capital expenditure of an entirely new manufacturing line. We integrate advanced servo drives, precision depositing heads, and energy-efficient automation seamlessly into your existing conveyor frameworks.

The outcome: higher throughput, drastically reduced giveaway rates, and flexible versatility for innovative recipes at optimized capital outlay.
